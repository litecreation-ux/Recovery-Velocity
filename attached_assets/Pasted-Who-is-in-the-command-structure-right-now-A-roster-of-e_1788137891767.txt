Who is in the command structure right now. A roster of every person and organization currently active in this incident — their name, role, agency, contact, when they joined, and their current status (Active, Standby, Offline). Grouped by ICS function: Command Staff, OPS Section, Logistics Section, Field Units, Unified Command Partners.

Jurisdictional boundaries and handoffs. When multiple agencies are involved — say ODPEM nationally, the St. Thomas Parish Disaster Committee locally, and JDF (Jamaica Defence Force) for logistics — Unified Command defines who has authority over what. The platform should show which agency owns which geographic zone or function, and where the boundaries are.

Shared Incident Objectives. In real ICS, Unified Command produces a single set of incident objectives that all agencies agree to operate under. On the platform this would be a pinned list — for example: Objective 1: Restore fuel supply to Portland and Westmoreland within 48 hours. Objective 2: Confirm shelter capacity for 2,400 displaced residents by end of day. Objective 3: Restore communications coverage to St. Andrew northern corridor. Every member of Unified Command sees these and all actions in the Incident Action Plan trace back to one of these objectives.

Resource sharing agreements. When a partner organization joins Unified Command they declare what they are contributing — personnel, equipment, facilities — and what they need in return. The platform captures this as a formal mutual aid record rather than a verbal agreement.

Communication log between agencies. A shared message thread visible to all Unified Command members — not individual chat but formal operational communications. Every message is timestamped and attributed. This feeds the AuditLogger agent and becomes part of the immutable record.

External agency status. If CDEMA regional support has been requested, or if JDF has been activated, or if international NGOs like IFRC or WFP are operating in the area — their status and point of contact lives here. The platform shows whether their resources have been declared, verified, and deployed into the Logistics Section.

Invitation and access management. The Incident Commander sends an invitation with a predefined role. The invitee receives a link, joins the incident, and lands in the correct view automatically. The Commander can remove access or change roles at any time. Every access change is logged.
