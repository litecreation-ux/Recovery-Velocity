Add a fifth role called Private Sector Partner to the existing role switcher. This implements the Community Resource Exchange layer — the most distinctive feature of the platform — where businesses, NGOs, hotels, logistics companies, and community organizations declare what resources they have so the coordination team knows what is actually available island-wide, not just what government holds.

Private Sector Partner view has two sections:

The first is a resource declaration form. The organization fills in: organization name, organization type (dropdown — Logistics & Transport, Hotel & Hospitality, Food & Retail, Fuel & Energy, Medical & Pharmacy, Construction & Equipment, Fishing & Maritime, Financial & Banking, NGO & Faith Community, Other), parish location, and then a list of resources they are offering. Each resource entry has: resource type (dropdown — Fuel, Food & Water, Shelter Beds, Vehicles & Trucks, Boats & Vessels, Medical Supplies, Generators, Communications Equipment, Personnel & Volunteers, Warehouse Space, Cash Funding), quantity, unit (litres, meals, beds, vehicles, people, etc.), and availability window (Immediately, Within 6 hours, Within 24 hours, Within 48 hours). They can add multiple resource rows. On submit, the declaration goes to POST /api/resources/declare on the backend and stores in memory with a timestamp and a generated organization ID.

The second section is a live feed showing all declarations submitted so far from other organizations, grouped by parish and resource type, so a partner can see what others have already pledged and avoid duplication.

On the backend, create GET /api/resources/available which returns all current declarations. Create a new AI agent called ResourceExchangeAgent that runs every 30 seconds. It reads all available private sector declarations and cross-references them against the current forecast data and Incident Command queue needs. When it finds a match — for example a logistics company in Kingston has 10 trucks available immediately and Route B1 clearance is the top bottleneck — it generates a recommendation and posts it to the Incident Command queue automatically as a new item flagged as "Resource Match." The recommendation text should read like: "Grace Kennedy Logistics (Kingston) has declared 10 trucks available immediately. Route B1 clearance would allow delivery to Portmore shelter within 4 hours. Recommend coordinator contact to confirm deployment."

On the National Coordinator and Parish Manager views, add a Resource Exchange panel showing a live count of declared resources by category across all parishes — total shelter beds available, total fuel litres declared, total food parcels, total vehicles. This updates every 30 seconds as new declarations come in. Each category is clickable and expands to show which organizations declared what and where.

The ResourceExchangeAgent should also appear in the Agent Monitor view alongside the existing agents, showing its last action and which parishes it is actively matching resources for.
